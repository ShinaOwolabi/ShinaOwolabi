## About Me

I am a Registered Animal Scientist (RAS/NIAS/02923), researcher, educator, and community development advocate. My work connects rigorous scientific investigations with practical field experience and a genuine commitment to using knowledge to improve lives.

I hold a **First Class Honours B.Tech in Animal Production** from the Federal University of Technology Minna, a Professional Diploma in Science Education, and an **MSc in Agricultural Biochemistry and Nutrition** from the University of Ibadan, completed in 2025. Across every level of study, I graduated top of my class, earning multiple national scholarships and commendation awards.

With over **12 peer-reviewed publications** in animal nutrition, poultry science, and agricultural biochemistry, including a book chapter published by CRC Press (Taylor & Francis Group), I bring both academic depth and practical research experience to every project.

In education, I served two years as a **Teach For Nigeria Fellow**, teaching Agricultural Science in underserved secondary schools in Ogun State. As **Volunteer Coordinator and Facilitator** at the Dreams to Legacy Initiative, I have mentored over 200 Nigerian youths on accessing quality education and career opportunities.

> *"Science without purpose is incomplete. I research, write, and teach because I believe knowledge applied well changes communities."*
